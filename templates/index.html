About the math

A fair coin has a probability of ½ for each side, and every flip is independent: the coin has no memory. With only a few flips, results can look lopsided and long streaks are common. The Law of Large Numbers says that as the number of flips grows, the observed share of heads gets closer and closer to 50%.

The chart below was made with Python and matplotlib (analysis.py): five independent runs of 10,000 flips each. Notice how wild they are at the start and how tightly they converge by the end.
